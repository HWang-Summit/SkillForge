# Daily knowledge ownership

Keep the existing research hierarchy for paper sources. Formal non-paper
knowledge has a visible Daily Knowledge entry in the main wiki Graph.
This changes knowledge navigation only, not source-area provenance or storage.

## Routing and reuse

- Determine the branch from the final approved raw source path. `raw/papers/`
  remains research; `raw/notes/`, `raw/articles/`, `raw/books/`, and other formal
  non-paper roots explicitly admitted by project SCHEMA are daily sources.
- Identify actual paper PDFs and run the existing Bridge route before wiki
  writing; never bypass it because the initial input was outside papers.
  Assets, hidden/workflow directories, inbox, and MinerU resources are not
  daily source pages. Unknown source roots need schema/user clarification.
- `wiki/domains/daily-knowledge.md` is one stable entry, not a new Domain per
  source format. Source-area pages stay provenance-only and filtered out of
  the global knowledge Graph.
- Prefer an existing relevant daily Topic. A new Topic needs multiple sources
  or one source plus reusable concept/entity knowledge; do not make one per note.
  Initial tools/environments, knowledge-base maintenance, AI agent workflows,
  and image generation Topics are examples, not a closed future taxonomy.

## Fallback and promotion

- Each daily summary and its relevant concept/entity knowledge links through a
  confirmed daily Topic to Daily Knowledge, or directly to the Daily Domain.
- With no trustworthy daily Topic, add a direct body/See also backlink and a
  reciprocal entry under `## 待整理内容` in Daily Knowledge. This is unorganized
  but not unowned; do not hide it, force a taxonomy, or create an empty Topic.
- When a Topic becomes justified, create/reuse its Domain and knowledge-page
  links first, then remove only that page's fallback backlink and pending-list
  entry. Preserve other factual content and meaningful links. Repeat maintenance
  must not duplicate pages or links.
- Shared concepts/entities can keep research links and add relevant daily links;
  no exclusive classification or file relocation is imposed. For cross-domain
  material with only a research Topic, retain a Daily Domain fallback unless a
  trustworthy daily route also exists. Do not alter the research Topic to force
  a personal classification.

## Maintenance

- Use actual body internal links. Plain `related` strings, tags, source-index,
  and source-area edges alone do not prove Daily Domain ownership.
- Keep original content language. New Chinese navigation headings do not imply
  translating English raw or summary bodies.
- Navigation-only repair updates changed wiki pages, index entries/dates/count,
  and append-only log; do not re-ingest unchanged sources or mutate raw,
  manifest, source-index, source-areas, Bridge, Zotero, Graph settings, or Canvas.
- Real new ingest continues normal provenance/source-area maintenance for the
  selected source. No background watcher or whole-library mirror is introduced.
